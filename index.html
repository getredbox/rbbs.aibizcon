What You'll Learn

The Four Pillars of Modern Business Technology

Whether you're a C-SUITE, CISO, CTO, MSP, MSSP, VAR, Tech Integrator, Risk Officer, or Technology Decision Maker, this dynamic event arms you with the knowledge to navigate an increasingly complex organizational landscape.

01

Artificial Intelligence Governance

Understanding AI policy frameworks, responsible deployment, bias mitigation, and building internal AI governance structures that protect your organization.

02

Cybersecurity Resilience + Controls

Practical strategies to strengthen your security posture, implement resilient controls, and prepare for evolving threat landscapes across your infrastructure.

03

Regulatory Frameworks & Compliance

Deep dives into PCI, NIST CSF, GLBA, CMMC, and HIPAA — demystifying what each framework demands and how to achieve and maintain compliance efficiently.

04

Leadership + Partners + Procurement

How to engage with partners, lead a team around your mission and procure technology in today's rapidly evolving marketplace where AI data and risk lead business decisions.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before attending. Can't find your answer? Reach out at aibizcon.com.

When should I register for this event?

+
AI conference tickets in the Bay Area cost an average of $1,850, this powerful technology event is an outstanding value and tickets will sell out soon, so early registration is recommended to secure your seat.

Who should attend this event?

+
This event is built for CISOs, CTOs, IT Directors, Managed Service Providers, Value Added Resellers, Technology Integrators, risk and compliance officers, security engineers, and anyone in a technology leadership or decision-making role across the San Francisco Bay Area.

Where exactly is this event?

+
Located in the Bay Area's agricultural jewel, Brentwood. As a center of innovation, logistics, infrastructure and remarkable growth opportunities; The City of Brentwood is an ideal location in Contra Costa County. The Conference will be held at Community Center Headquarters — 35 Oak Street, Brentwood, CA.

Will sessions be recorded or available on-demand?

+
Select sessions may be made available following the event. Registered attendees will receive information about post-event materials via email. We strongly encourage in-person attendance for the full experience.

What compliance frameworks will be covered?

+
Sessions will cover AI Governance Frameworks as well as PCI, NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF), GLBA, CMMC, and HIPPA. The agenda is designed to provide strategic overviews as well as practical implementation guidance.

How do I become an event sponsor?

+
We offer Gold, Silver, and Bronze sponsorship tiers with varying levels of visibility, speaking opportunities, and exhibition space. Email info@aibizcon.com or WOW@GETREDBOX.com, or visit aibizcon.com to request a sponsorship prospectus.

Will there be CPE or CEU credits available?

+
We are working to have this event accredited for Continuing Professional Education (CPE) credits applicable to CISSP, CISA, CISM and other security certifications. Details will be confirmed and communicated to registered attendees as options evolve.

Can I bring colleagues or team members?

+
Absolutely — we encourage teams to attend together. Each individual must register separately due to capacity limits. Group registration inquiries can be sent to info@aibizcon.com or WOW@GETREDBOX.com.

Still have questions? Visit aibizcon.com or email info@aibizcon.com
