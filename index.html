Three Pillars of Modern Tech Compliance

Whether you're a CISO, CTO, risk officer, or technology decision maker, this event arms you with the knowledge to navigate an increasingly complex compliance landscape.

01

Artificial Intelligence Governance

Understanding AI policy frameworks, responsible deployment, bias mitigation, and building internal AI governance structures that protect your organization.

02

Cybersecurity Resilience + Controls

Practical strategies to strengthen your security posture, implement resilient controls, and prepare for evolving threat landscapes across your infrastructure.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before attending. Can't find your answer? Reach out at ccbizcon.com.

Is this event free to attend?

+
Yes — CCBIZCON is a free event for qualified technology professionals and decision makers. Simply register in advance to secure your spot, as seating is limited.

Who should attend this event?

+
This event is built for CISOs, CTOs, IT directors, risk and compliance officers, security engineers, and anyone in a technology leadership or decision-making role across the San Francisco Bay Area.

Where exactly is the venue in Brentwood?

+
The precise venue address will be provided upon registration confirmation. The event is held in the City of Brentwood in the East Bay. Complimentary parking is available on site. Visit ccbizcon.com for updates.

Will sessions be recorded or available on-demand?

+
Select sessions may be made available following the event. Registered attendees will receive information about post-event materials via email. We strongly encourage in-person attendance for the full experience.

What compliance frameworks will be covered?

+
Sessions will cover PCI DSS, NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF), GLBA, CMMC, and HIPAA, as well as emerging AI governance frameworks. The agenda is designed to give both strategic overviews and practical implementation guidance.

How do I become an event sponsor?

+
We offer Gold, Silver, and Bronze sponsorship tiers with varying levels of visibility, speaking opportunities, and exhibition space. Email info@ccbizcon.com or visit ccbizcon.com to request a sponsorship prospectus.

Will there be CPE or CEU credits available?

+
We are working to have this event accredited for Continuing Professional Education (CPE) credits applicable to CISSP, CISA, CISM, and other security certifications. Details will be confirmed and communicated to registered attendees.

Can I bring colleagues or team members?

+
Absolutely — we encourage teams to attend together. Each individual must register separately due to capacity limits. Group registration inquiries can be sent to info@ccbizcon.com.

Still have questions? Visit ccbizcon.com or email info@ccbizcon.com
